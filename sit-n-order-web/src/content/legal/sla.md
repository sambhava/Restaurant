### How we prioritise

When several things need attention at once, we work in this order:

1. **Service down** — nobody can take orders. Dropped everything else.
2. **Something broken but workable** — one feature failing while orders still flow.
3. **Questions and requests** — how do I, can you add.

A restaurant mid-service comes before one that is closed. If you are in service and stuck, say so in the first line of your message.

## 2. Availability

We aim for **[UPTIME_GOAL] uptime**, measured monthly, excluding the situations in section 3.

**This is a goal, not a guarantee.** There are no service credits, no financial penalties, and no automatic refunds tied to it. What you get instead: if a confirmed fault on our side makes the service substantially unusable for more than 72 consecutive hours in a billing month, you can claim a full refund for that month under the [Refund Policy](/legal/refund).

We would rather write that down plainly than promise a number we cannot enforce against our own suppliers.

## 3. What isn't counted

Availability excludes time lost to:

- **Planned maintenance**, announced at least 24 hours ahead, kept to quiet hours where possible.
- **Outages at Google Firebase or Vercel.** These host the service; when they go down, we go down, and we have no more ability to fix it than you do.
- **Your own connectivity, devices, printers, or power.**
- **Suspension** for non-payment or a breach of the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).
- **Force majeure** — anything genuinely beyond reasonable control.

## 4. What we do about incidents

If the service is down:

- We investigate as soon as we know, and you can tell us on WhatsApp.
- We tell you what we know, including when we do not yet know the cause.
- Once resolved, we say what happened, in plain terms.

We would rather say "Firebase is down in our region and we are waiting on Google" than go quiet.

## 5. Your data during an outage

Orders already saved are not lost when the service is unreachable. They live in the database, not on the device.

The diner-facing menu shows an offline notice rather than failing silently, and reconnects on its own.

**Have a paper fallback.** Any kitchen that depends entirely on one system is one outage away from a bad evening — that is true of every ordering product, not just this one.

## 6. Backups

Data is stored in Google Firestore, which replicates across data centres automatically. That protects against hardware failure.

**It does not protect against you deleting something by mistake.** If you delete a menu item or close a bill in error, we may not be able to recover it. Ask us early rather than late — the sooner you tell us, the better the chance.

## 7. Changes and support for old versions

We update the software regularly. Updates arrive automatically; there is nothing to install.

We will give notice before any change that alters how something you rely on works.
