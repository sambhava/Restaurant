**In short.** This website uses only what it needs to work. We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell anything about your visit.

## 1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. Some are essential — a site cannot keep you logged in without one. Others track you across sites for advertising. We use the first kind and not the second.

"Local storage" works similarly and is covered here too.

## 2. What this website uses

| What | Purpose | Type | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firebase authentication token | Keeps you signed in | Essential | Until you sign out |
| Session state | Remembers where you are in the signup form | Essential | Until the tab closes |

That is the complete list for this website.

## 3. What the diner-facing menu uses

When someone scans a QR code at your table, their browser stores:

| What | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous sign-in token | Lets them place an order without registering | Until the browser data is cleared |
| Table session reference | Keeps their order attached to their table while they eat | Until the table's bill is closed |
| Theme preference | Remembers light or dark, if they change it | Until cleared |

None of these identifies the diner personally. There is no name, phone number, or email in any of them.

## 4. What we do not use

We do not use:

- Advertising or retargeting cookies.
- Third-party analytics such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel.
- Social media tracking pixels.
- Cross-site tracking of any kind.

This is why you will not find a cookie consent banner here. Under Indian and EU rules, strictly necessary cookies do not require consent, and we do not set any others. If that changes, we will ask first.

## 5. Controlling cookies

Your browser can block or delete cookies — usually under Settings, then Privacy.

Be aware that blocking essential cookies will stop you signing in, and will stop diners placing orders, because the software has no other way to remember who is who between one page and the next.

## 6. Changes

If we start using anything beyond what is listed here, we will update this page and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before setting it.
