**What this covers.** We handle two different kinds of personal data: yours, as the restaurant that signs up, and your diners', when they order. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 1. Who is responsible for what

For **your** data as a restaurant — your name, contact details, business details — we are the Data Fiduciary. We decide what to collect and why.

For **your diners'** data — what they ordered, at which table, when — **you** are the Data Fiduciary and we are your Data Processor. We handle it on your instructions, through the software, and for no purpose of our own. The [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) sets out those terms.

This distinction matters: if a diner asks about their order history, that request is yours to answer, and we will help you do it.

## 2. What we collect from you

**When you register:**

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Restaurant name, city, state | To set up your account and address your invoices |
| Your name | So we know who we're dealing with |
| Email address | Your login, and how we reach you |
| Mobile number | Support, and WhatsApp if you opted in |
| Number of outlets and tables | To configure your account |
| FSSAI licence number (optional) | To print on your bills, where food-safety rules require it |
| GSTIN (optional) | To issue you a correct tax invoice |
| Preferred language | So we reply in a language you're comfortable with |

**When you use the dashboard:** your login times, and the actions you take in the software, so we can support you and investigate problems.

**Your consent choices**, each recorded separately with the date and time you gave it.

We do not collect payment card details. Payment happens by bank transfer or UPI, outside this website.

## 3. What the service collects from your diners

When someone scans a QR code and orders:

- The table number, from the QR code itself.
- What they ordered, including variants, add-ons, and any note they typed.
- Order times and status changes.
- An anonymous session identifier, so their order stays on their phone while they eat.

**We do not ask diners for their name, phone number, or email.** A diner orders without creating an account, and nothing in the ordering flow requires them to identify themselves.

## 4. Why we use it

- To provide the service you pay for.
- To activate your account after payment.
- To contact you about your account, service problems, or changes to these policies.
- To send product updates — **only if you opted in**, and you can stop at any time.
- To meet our legal and tax obligations.
- To investigate misuse or a security incident.

We do not sell your data. We do not sell your diners' data. We do not use either to train machine-learning models.

## 5. Menu photographs

Images you upload for menu items are compressed and stored directly in the database as encoded image data, rather than in separate file storage.

Two consequences worth knowing:

- Anyone who can read your menu — including diners with your QR code — can see these images. That is the point of them, but do not upload anything you would not want a customer to see.
- Very large image libraries make your menu slower to load. Keep photographs to what you actually need.

## 6. Who else is involved

We use a small number of suppliers to run the service. Each processes data only to provide their service to us:

| Supplier | What they do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase | Stores your data, handles logins | Google Cloud data centres |
| Vercel | Serves the website and dashboard | Global edge network |
| Resend | Sends account emails | Cloud infrastructure |

These providers may store or process data outside India. Where that happens, we rely on their contractual data protection commitments. If you need your data kept solely within India, tell us before signing up — we cannot promise it with the current setup.

We will also disclose data if a law, court, or regulator validly requires it. Where we are permitted to tell you, we will.

## 7. How long we keep things

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Your account and restaurant details | While your account is active, then 30 days |
| Orders, sessions, and menu data | While your account is active, then 30 days |
| Invoices and payment records | 8 years, as tax law requires |
| Signup submissions that were never activated | 12 months |
| Consent records | As long as the account, then 3 years, as evidence of what was agreed |

After the retention period we delete the data. Ask us and we will delete sooner, except where we are legally required to keep it.

## 8. Keeping it secure

- Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. **We never see or store your password**, and no one at our end can read it.
- Database rules enforce that each restaurant can reach only its own data. A signed-in account cannot read another restaurant's menu, orders, or revenue.
- Administrative functions are separately protected.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

## 9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

- **Ask what we hold** about you and how we use it.
- **Correct** anything inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Ask us to delete** your data, where we are not legally required to keep it.
- **Withdraw a consent** you gave. Withdrawing consent for something essential to the service — such as processing needed to run your account — means we can no longer provide it.
- **Nominate someone** to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
- **Complain** to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve it.

To exercise any of these, email our Grievance Officer below. We respond within 30 days.
