**What this is for.** When your diners order, personal data about them passes through our software. Under India's DPDP Act 2023 you are the Data Fiduciary for that data and we are your Data Processor. This document records the terms on which we process it. It forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and applies automatically — you do not need to sign anything separately.

## 1. Roles

**You** decide what data is collected from your diners and why. You are the Data Fiduciary.

**We** process that data only to provide the software, on your instructions. We are the Data Processor.

Where we process **your own** data as a customer — your contact details, your billing information — we act as Data Fiduciary in our own right, and the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) governs that.

## 2. What we process on your behalf

**Categories of data:**

- Order contents: items, variants, add-ons, quantities, and any note the diner typed.
- Table number and session identifiers.
- Order timestamps and status changes.
- An anonymous authentication identifier generated for the diner's browser.

**Categories of data subject:** your diners, and your staff who hold logins.

**Note:** the software does not collect diners' names, phone numbers, email addresses, or payment details. If you add such information to an order note, you become responsible for having a lawful basis to do so.

**Purpose:** to receive, display, track, and bill orders, and to produce your analytics.

**Duration:** while your account is active, plus 30 days after it closes.

## 3. What we undertake

We will:

- **Process only on your instructions.** Using the software as intended is your instruction. We will not process your diners' data for any purpose of our own — no resale, no advertising, no model training.
- **Keep it confidential.** Anyone with access is bound by confidentiality obligations.
- **Keep it secure**, as described in section 5.
- **Not engage a new sub-processor** without telling you first (section 4).
- **Help you respond** to a diner exercising their rights, at no extra charge for reasonable requests.
- **Tell you about a breach** without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware, with what we know and what we are doing.
- **Delete or return the data** when the account closes, per section 6.
- **Give you the information you reasonably need** to demonstrate we are meeting these obligations.

## 4. Sub-processors

We use these, and only these:

| Sub-processor | What they do | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud / Firebase | Database, authentication, hosting | Google data centres |
| Vercel Inc. | Application hosting and delivery | Global edge network |
| Resend | Transactional email to you (not your diners) | Cloud infrastructure |

We will give you at least 30 days' notice by email before adding or replacing a sub-processor. If you object on reasonable data-protection grounds, tell us within those 30 days; if we cannot resolve it, you may cancel without penalty and receive a pro-rata refund of anything paid in advance.

## 5. Security measures

- Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) for all traffic.
- Encryption at rest, as provided by Google Cloud.
- Database security rules that enforce tenant isolation — a signed-in account can reach only its own restaurant's data.
- Authentication handled by Firebase. Passwords are never stored in readable form and are not visible to us.
- Administrative functions separately access-controlled.
- Access limited to those who need it to operate the service.

We may improve these measures over time. We will not weaken them.

## 6. When the account ends

We keep the data for **30 days** after closure so you can request an export. After that we delete it from live systems.

Backups may retain copies for a short further period as they age out on their normal cycle. Those copies are not accessible for ordinary use and are deleted as the backups rotate.

Ask us and we will delete sooner, except where we must keep something to meet a legal obligation.

## 7. International transfers

Our sub-processors may store or process data outside India. Where they do, we rely on the contractual data-protection commitments in their terms.

If your circumstances require data to remain within India, raise it with us before signing up. We cannot currently guarantee it, and we would rather tell you that now.

## 8. Audits

If you reasonably need to verify our compliance, email us and we will provide what we can — a written description of our measures, and the security documentation our sub-processors publish.

We are a small operation and cannot host on-site audits. If your own regulatory position requires one, tell us before signing up so we can be straight with you about whether we are a suitable supplier.
