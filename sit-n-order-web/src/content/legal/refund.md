**In short.** You pay monthly, in advance. Cancel whenever you like and you keep access until the month you paid for runs out. We do not refund part-months. If we fail to activate your account after you have paid, you get all of it back.

## 1. How billing works

The subscription is **[PRICE] per outlet, per month**, paid in advance by bank transfer or UPI.

Because payment is manual rather than through a card gateway, there is no automatic renewal and no standing instruction on your account. You pay each month; if you stop paying, the service stops. Nothing is ever charged without you actively sending it.

## 2. Cancelling

Email us and say you want to stop. That is the whole process — no notice period, no cancellation fee, no form.

Your account stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that it is suspended and no further payment is due.

## 3. When we refund

**We refund in full if:**

- You paid and we never activated your account.
- We closed your account for a reason that was not your fault.
- You were charged twice for the same month, or charged after cancelling.
- A confirmed fault on our side made the service substantially unusable for more than 72 consecutive hours in a billing month, and you tell us within that month.

Refunds go back by the same route you paid, within 7 working days of us agreeing to one.

## 4. When we don't refund

**We do not refund:**

- Part of a month, if you cancel mid-month. You keep access to the end of it instead.
- Months already used, if you decide the product is not for you.
- Downtime caused by something outside our control — your internet connection, your device, your power supply, or an outage at Google Firebase or Vercel.
- An account closed because of a serious breach of the [Terms](/legal/terms) or [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

## 5. Try it before you commit

We would rather you found out the product doesn't suit you before paying than argue about a refund afterwards.

Ask us for a walkthrough before you sign up. We will show you the dashboard with sample data and answer questions. It costs you nothing and takes about twenty minutes.

## 6. If you disagree with a charge

Email us with the details. We will look into it and reply within 2 working days.

We would much rather sort out a billing disagreement directly than have you out of pocket. If we have made a mistake, we will say so and fix it.

## 7. Price changes

If the subscription price changes, we will tell you at least 30 days in advance by email. The new price applies from your next payment. You may cancel instead of accepting it, and nothing you have already paid is affected.
