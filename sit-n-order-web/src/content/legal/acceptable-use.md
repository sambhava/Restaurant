**In short.** Run your restaurant with it, honestly and lawfully. Don't misrepresent your food, don't go looking through other people's data, and don't use it for anything illegal.

## 1. Who this applies to

You, and everyone you give a login to. You are responsible for what your staff do under your account, so make sure they know these rules.

## 2. Your food, described honestly

Your menu is what diners rely on to decide what to eat. It must be accurate.

**In particular, the veg and non-veg indicators must be correct.** Large numbers of diners in India avoid non-vegetarian food for religious or dietary reasons, and many will trust a green mark without asking further. Marking a non-vegetarian dish as vegetarian is a serious breach of this policy and grounds for immediate closure of your account. It may also breach food-safety law.

You must also:

- Describe dishes, portions, and variants accurately.
- Price items at what you will actually charge.
- Not use photographs of food you do not serve in a way that misleads.
- Declare allergens where the law requires it.

## 3. Follow the law that applies to you

You are responsible for your own compliance with:

- **FSSAI** licensing and food-safety rules, including displaying your licence number where required.
- **GST** and other tax obligations on your sales. Our software calculates GST at the rate you configure — verifying that it is the correct rate is your responsibility, not ours.
- Local licensing, weights and measures, and consumer protection law.
- Employment law, for the staff you give access to.

## 4. Don't attack the service

Do not:

- Try to access another restaurant's data, or any part of the system you have not been given access to.
- Probe, scan, or test the security of the service without our written permission.
- Attempt to bypass authentication, rate limits, or database rules.
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, or copy the software.
- Automate the service in ways that place unreasonable load on it.
- Introduce malware, or use the service to distribute it.

**If you find a security flaw, tell us.** Email [EMAIL] with what you found. We will not pursue anyone who reports a genuine vulnerability in good faith and gives us a fair chance to fix it before disclosing it. We would much rather hear from you than from a customer.

## 5. Don't misuse other people's data

- Do not upload personal data about your diners that you have no lawful basis to hold.
- Do not use order data to contact diners in ways they have not agreed to.
- Do not share your login with people outside your business.
- Do not export data and use it for something unrelated to running your restaurant.

## 6. Content you upload

Anything you upload — menu photographs, descriptions, your logo — must be yours to use, or properly licensed. Do not upload anything unlawful, obscene, or that infringes someone else's rights.

Remember menu photographs are visible to anyone with your QR code.

## 7. Don't resell it as your own

Do not resell, sublicense, rent, or white-label the service without our written agreement. If you want to offer it to other restaurants, talk to us — that is a conversation we are happy to have, on the right terms.

## 8. What happens if you breach this

Depending on how serious it is:

- **A warning**, for something minor or apparently accidental. Most issues end here.
- **Suspension**, until it is put right.
- **Immediate closure**, without refund, for something serious — misrepresenting veg status, attacking the service, or using it for something illegal.

We will tell you what the problem is and, where the situation allows, give you a chance to fix it. Where continuing would put your diners or us at legal risk, we may act immediately.

## 9. Reporting a problem

If you believe another user is breaching this policy, email [EMAIL] with the details. We treat reports confidentially.

## 10. Changes

We will update this page if the rules change, and email you if a change materially affects what you may do.
